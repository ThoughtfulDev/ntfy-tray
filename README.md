## A calmer notification flow

ntfy-tray lives quietly in the menu bar, keeping your ntfy messages close without demanding attention. Choose the topics you care about, catch up in a focused Inbox, and take a break whenever you need one.

Built to feel at home on the Mac: clear, private, and out of the way until it is useful.

## At a glance

- A dedicated place for topic-based notifications
- A clean Inbox for catching up at your own pace
- Do Not Disturb when you want less noise
- Designed as a native macOS companion
